Gated Media Access demos

Each demo opens a complete WordPress site in your browser with WordPress Playground, with the plugin installed and sample content ready. Nothing is installed on your computer, and the site is gone when you close the tab.

Every site opens with a Start here page listing what to try. Customer accounts use the password demo; the administrator is admin / password.

Customer account area

Signed in as Sam, a customer

My Access, Files, Orders and Profile for someone holding a year of the onboarding course, market reports bought with a coupon, and a post about to run out.

Administrator screens

Signed in as the administrator

The Access list, Add Access, Groups, Products, Coupons, Payments and Settings, with access given by hand, bought, claimed free and sent by another system.

Getting access free

Signed in as Sam, holding nothing yet

Claim a free product, then use a 100% coupon on a priced one, and see both in the account area. Neither needs Stripe.

Restricted as a visitor

Not signed in

Restricted posts give a 404 and are left out of listings and search, restricted files are refused, and product pages ask you to sign in to buy.
